Stake $RUNGS

Fees in, ETH out, over 7 days

  1. 1
    When a ladder owner claims swap fees, the LadderManager keeps a fixed 5% of the ETH side of those fees. Fees in USDG or any other token go 100% to the owner. Never principal; the rate is set at deploy and cannot change.
  2. 2
    That ETH goes straight to the StaggerStake contract. It swaps nothing and holds no other token; it only receives ETH.
  3. 3
    ETH is streamed to $RUNGS stakers pro rata over 7 days. Stake, unstake and claim whenever you like; there is no lock.

No promised returns. If no ETH fees are claimed, nothing flows. Details in the docs.
