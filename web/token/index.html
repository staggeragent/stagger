What the token does

  • Stake for the ETH fee stream. When an owner claims swap fees, the LadderManager keeps a fixed 5% of the ETH side only (set at deploy, never on principal; fees in USDG or other tokens go 100% to the owner). That ETH goes to the StaggerStake contract, which streams it to stakers over 7 days, pro rata to stake.
  • No lock. Stake, unstake and claim whenever you like.
  • No vote, no admin. The token controls nothing in the contracts. There is no owner key that can change the fee or move funds.
No promised returns. The stream is only as large as the ETH fees people actually claim (pools without ETH add nothing). If nobody opens ladders or claims fees, stakers receive nothing. $RUNGS can lose all of its value. Nothing here is financial advice.
